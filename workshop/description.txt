[h1]Faster Washing & Reduced Soap Consumption [B42][/h1]

[b]The apocalypse is bad enough. You don't need to spend half your day washing your clothes.[/b]

This mod lets you configure clothing-washing time, personal-washing time, solid-soap consumption, and liquid-cleaner consumption independently in Sandbox Options.

[b]Default multipliers:[/b]

Clothing washing: 0.5x
Personal washing: 0.5x
Solid soap consumption: 0.02x
Liquid cleaner consumption: 0.02x

No new items, recipes, crafting systems, or dependencies. Designed for Project Zomboid Build 42.22.2.
